Why the ending is a separate sheet

The first attempt put it inside 08, over the event log it had just written. Measured, it failed for a reason worth recording: the register and the log are the same kind of thing at the same scale — mono text in rows — so one landed on top of the other and read as a rendering fault rather than as a card. Deepening the wash traded the fault for a different one, a slide covering a product that was still trying to be legible underneath it. An ending needs its own compositing plane; in product that plane keeps the live desktop rather than replacing it with a black card.

It also buys the thing the sequence was missing: the beat list gets an ending it can name. The runner plays beats, not files, so `09:account` and `09:lockup` are now orderable pieces like every other — and the flow's last frame is a held card rather than whatever the last scene happened to stop on.

The account becomes the mark

04 states its own device plainly: the mark is drawn BY the light, not revealed under it — it runs out along the stem, splits into three, and the three branches are the paths it takes. One input becomes three, and the motion is the only thing that says so. The close uses the same direction because direction is part of the mark, not an interchangeable transition.

Seven lights, one per thing the run produced, first travel inward and become the single input. Only then does that one open into three. The claim is not decoration: a fleet's work coming back as one thing the project keeps is what the last two beats of 08 actually did, and this is that sentence drawn.
